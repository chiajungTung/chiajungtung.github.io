About

I completed my PhD in environmental epidemiology at National Taiwan University in 2026, based in Professor Pau-Chung Chen's Precision Environmental Medicine Lab. My dissertation examined how early-life chemical exposures — including parabens and PFAS — relate to children's neurodevelopment, drawing on the Taiwan Birth Panel Study (TBPS), a long-running birth-cohort platform I also helped advance methodologically.

I'm currently a Visiting Scholar in Marc Weisskopf's lab at Harvard T.H. Chan School of Public Health, using the St. Louis Baby Tooth Study to examine how early-life lead exposure relates to adult cognitive and educational outcomes.
